Our Purpose

To give Land Rover owners the data and context they need to make confident decisions about their engine.

Whether you're facing a crankshaft failure on an SDV6, a timing chain on an Ingenium, or just trying to decide between TDV6 vs SDV6, we believe the answer should be based on evidence — not forum scaremongering or a sales pitch.

That's why we built this site around real enquiry data from EnginesMarket.co.uk and validated it against manufacturer TSBs, independent workshops, and post‑repair surveys. The result is a resource that tells you what actually fails, what it costs, and whether it's worth fixing — all in plain English.

We also publish our editorial standards and data verification so you can judge the claims for yourself.

How We Work

Three steps: research, analyse, connect.

  1. Research — We collect enquiry data, supplier quotes, post-sale feedback, and cross‑reference with public sources and manufacturer TSBs.
  2. Analyse — Every engine, failure mode, and cost band is tagged with its source ([EM-VERIFIED], [EM-QUOTE], [PATTERN DATA]). We identify patterns and publish them openly.
  3. Connect — When you request a quote, we route your enquiry only to specialists who meet our published vetting criteria. We never sell your data or spam you.

Our Specialist Network & Vetting

We don't recommend garages based on who pays us the most. We recommend them based on who meets our published criteria.

We currently work with 12 vetted JLR specialists across the UK. Every one of them has passed a seven‑point checklist that includes:

  • Genuine JLR specialism (regular work on Land Rover, Range Rover, and Jaguar powertrains)
  • Minimum trading history with verifiable premises and insurance
  • A written warranty on both the unit and the labour
  • Engine provenance — they can state where a unit came from and to what standard it was rebuilt
  • Consistent response times and documented complaint handling

We also have four automatic disqualifiers — including no written warranty, quoting without diagnosis, and unresolved complaint patterns. See the full list on our specialist vetting page.

Specialists are reviewed on an ongoing basis, not just at the point of joining. If a specialist's outcomes drop, routing is reduced or stopped.

Our Editorial Independence

We are paid by specialists for enquiries, not by you. This is how we keep the site free and sustainable.

But we believe that commercial model only works if we tell you when not to replace an engine as clearly as when to do it. That's why we have a published repair/replace/scrap framework with a 60% threshold — if a reconditioned engine costs more than 60% of the vehicle's value, we say so plainly.

Our editorial team is independent of any supplier. We don't accept sponsored content, we don't promote specific specialists on the page, and we don't change our verdicts based on who pays. Our editorial standards are published for exactly this reason — so you can hold us to account.

And if we get something wrong, we correct it publicly — see our corrections log.

What We Are Not

We are not a garage, we are not a dealership, and we are not affiliated with Jaguar Land Rover Limited.

  • Not a garage — we don't fit engines, we don't hold stock, and we never present ourselves as the party doing the work.
  • Not a sales agent — we don't take a cut of the repair bill; we're paid per enquiry routed.
  • Not affiliated with JLR — we are an independent third party. Any mention of Land Rover, Range Rover, or Jaguar is for identification purposes only.
  • Not a forum — we don't offer DIY advice, and we never instruct you to perform unsafe checks.

If you're looking for a garage, we'll help you find one. If you're looking for a sales pitch, you won't find it here. For more detail, see our how it works page.

Why We Publish This

Because Land Rover ownership shouldn't be a leap of faith.

We've seen too many owners scrap a vehicle that could have been saved, or pour money into an engine that should have been retired. The difference is almost always good information — knowing what the real failure rate is, what a fair price looks like, and who to trust to do the work.

We publish our data, our methodology, and our verdicts because we believe that an informed owner is a better owner. And we keep the site free because the most useful resource is one that everyone can access.

If you find this useful, share it. If you find an error, tell us. And if you're ready to get a real quote from a vetted specialist, use the tools below.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge for quote requests?

No. The site is free for owners. Specialists pay us for the enquiries we send them, but you pay nothing — and we never charge a commission on the final repair.

How do I know the specialists are trustworthy?

We publish our full vetting criteria and disqualifiers. Every specialist in our network meets those standards, and we review them continuously. Read the full vetting criteria.

Are you affiliated with Land Rover or JLR?

No. We are independent and not affiliated with Jaguar Land Rover Limited or any official dealer network.

Where does your data come from?

Our primary data comes from 24,650 real UK quote requests collected in 2025, supplemented by supplier quotes, post‑sale surveys, mechanic interviews, and manufacturer TSBs. Everything is tagged by source so you can verify it.
