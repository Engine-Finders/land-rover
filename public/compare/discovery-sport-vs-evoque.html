If you need space, practicality, and seven‑seat flexibility, buy the Discovery Sport. If you want style, urban agility, and a more premium image, the Range Rover Evoque is the smarter choice. For families, the Discovery Sport is the clear winner; for urban drivers who value style over space, the Evoque remains a compelling choice.

The Verdict

The Discovery Sport is the better all‑rounder for most buyers. It offers more space, seven‑seat availability, and a more practical boot, while sharing the same engine line‑up as the Evoque. The Sport’s 362 enquiries in 2025 edge out the Evoque L551’s 349 [EM-VERIFIED], reflecting its broader appeal to families. However, the Evoque is the more stylish and desirable vehicle, with a premium interior and a lower, sportier stance. If you value practicality and value for money, the Discovery Sport wins; if you’re willing to compromise on space for style and prestige, the Evoque is the one to choose. Both carry the same 204DTD diesel engine risks, so service history is paramount [PATTERN DATA]. Replacement costs are similar, but the Sport’s larger parc means cheaper used engines are more readily available.

Range Rover Evoque Deep Dive

The Range Rover Evoque L551 (2019–present) is the second generation of Land Rover's stylish compact SUV. It’s available with the 204DTD 2.0‑litre diesel (D150, D180), the 204PT 2.0‑litre petrol (P200, P250), and a PHEV variant (P300e). The Evoque L538 (2011–2018) also used the 204DTD and the Ford‑sourced 224DT 2.2 diesel; our data shows 244 enquiries for the L538 [EM-VERIFIED], but the L551 is more popular. The 204DTD has a documented pre‑2019 timing chain tensioner weakness [PATTERN DATA], revised from the 2019 model year. Replacement costs for the 204DTD are £3,000–£5,800 supply and fit [EM-QUOTE], making it relatively affordable. The Evoque’s compact dimensions and lower roofline give it a sporty feel, but they also reduce rear headroom and boot space – a trade‑off for its stylish looks. Full engine specs and reliability ratings are on our 204DTD Knowledge Centre and 204PT Knowledge Centre. For earlier models with the 224DT, see the 224DT Knowledge Centre. The Engine Failure Index 2026 provides context on how these engines compare across the range.

Discovery Sport Deep Dive

The Discovery Sport L550 (2014–2019) and its facelift (2019–present) is Land Rover’s compact seven‑seat SUV. It shares the same 204DTD diesel, 204PT petrol, and early 224DT 2.2 diesel engines as the Evoque. The Sport’s longer wheelbase and taller roofline translate into significantly more rear legroom, a larger boot (up to 1,698 litres with seats folded) and an optional third row – a rarity in this class. The 204DTD timing chain risk applies to pre‑2019 units [PATTERN DATA], so post‑2019 models are safer. Replacement cost for the 204DTD is £3,500–£5,800 [EM-QUOTE], slightly higher than the Evoque due to the larger vehicle’s labour access, but still affordable. Our data shows 362 enquiries for the L550 [EM-VERIFIED], making it one of the most‑enquired models on the site. For full engine details, visit the 204DTD Knowledge Centre and the 224DT Knowledge Centre for earlier examples. The Discovery Sport Economics Guide breaks down the repair‑or‑replace decision.

Ownership Costs Compared

The biggest cost difference is the purchase price – a used Discovery Sport typically costs £1,000–£3,000 less than a comparable Evoque, thanks to its more utilitarian image. Running costs are similar: both share the same engines, so fuel economy (around 35–40 mpg for the 204DTD) and servicing costs are comparable. Insurance is slightly higher for the Evoque due to its premium positioning. Engine replacement costs are similar, with the Sport’s 204DTD at £3,500–£5,800 and the Evoque’s at £3,000–£5,800 [EM-QUOTE]. The Sport’s larger weight may slightly impact fuel economy and tyre wear, but the difference is marginal. The 60% threshold [EM-INTERVIEW] applies to both: for a Discovery Sport valued at £12,000, a £5,800 replacement is 48% – a ✅ Safe decision; for an Evoque valued at £14,000, a £5,800 replacement is 41% – also ✅ Safe. Which Should You Buy?

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Buyer / Needs Space

Choose the Discovery Sport. Its seven‑seat option, larger boot, and more generous rear legroom make it the clear winner for families. The Sport also offers better visibility and a more upright driving position.

🏙️

Urban Driver / Style Seeker

Pick the Range Rover Evoque. Its coupe‑like roofline, premium interior, and sharper handling make it more desirable and fun to drive. It’s easier to park and manoeuvre in city traffic.

💰

Value‑Conscious Buyer

The Discovery Sport offers better value for money – lower purchase price, similar running costs, and more practicality. If you don't need seven seats, the Sport is still the more cost‑effective choice.

🔁

Long‑Term Ownership

Both can reach high mileages with care. The Evoque’s stronger resale values make it a better investment if you sell after 3‑5 years. For a decade of ownership, the Sport’s cheaper used parts and wider availability may give it an edge.

Whichever you choose, the repair vs replace framework and the ownership cost calculator can help you decide whether to fix or move on when the bills arrive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is more reliable, Evoque or Discovery Sport?

Reliability is similar for models with the same engine – both use the 204DTD diesel, which has a known pre‑2019 timing chain risk. The Discovery Sport's larger weight may put slightly more strain on the drivetrain, but overall, maintenance history matters more than the model badge. Both are capable of high mileages when well‑maintained.

Is the Evoque more expensive to run than the Discovery Sport?

Running costs are broadly similar for same‑engine examples. The Evoque's higher purchase price and insurance premiums make it more expensive overall, but fuel and servicing costs are comparable. The Evoque's lower weight may give a marginal fuel economy advantage, but it's negligible.

Which has more space inside?

The Discovery Sport has significantly more space – it offers seven seats and a larger boot (up to 1,698 litres) compared to the Evoque's five seats and up to 1,383 litres. For families, the Sport is the clear winner on space.

Which holds its value better?

The Range Rover Evoque traditionally holds its value better due to its premium image and desirability. However, the Discovery Sport is also strong, and the gap has narrowed in recent years. For the best resale, choose a desirable engine and spec and maintain a full service history.

Which is better off‑road?

Both are capable off‑road, with full‑time four‑wheel drive, terrain response, and good ground clearance. The Discovery Sport's longer wheelbase gives it slightly better stability on steep descents, while the Evoque's shorter overhangs improve approach and departure angles. In practice, both are more than adequate for light off‑roading and green‑laning.
