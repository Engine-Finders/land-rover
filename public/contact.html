How the Contact & Enquiry Process Works

We built this process to remove friction and uncertainty from engine replacement. You are not left to cold-call garages or chase quotes across multiple sites. One enquiry reaches the right specialist, and you get a clear, itemised quote.

  1. Identify your engine — Use our registration lookup or engine code decoder to confirm which BMW engine is in your car.
  2. Submit your enquiry — Tell us what is happening (symptoms, fault codes, or simply "I need a replacement").
  3. We match you — Based on your engine code, location, and urgency, we route your enquiry to the most suitable specialist from our vetted network.
  4. You get a quote — Direct from the specialist, typically within 2–4 hours, with a breakdown of supply, fitting, and warranty terms [EM-INTERVIEW].
  5. You decide — No pressure, no obligation. The quote is yours to accept, compare, or decline.

We deliberately avoid gating the result behind an email address. You get the quote first; we ask for contact details only to put you in touch with the specialist.

How We Vet Every BMW Specialist

There is no "trust us" language here. The checklist below is what we actually verify before adding a specialist to our network. Every point is independently verified by our editorial team, not self-reported by the workshop [EM-INTERVIEW].

  • BMW-specific diagnostic equipment — Each specialist must hold and use ISTA, Rheingold, or equivalent dealer-level diagnostic tooling.
  • Minimum 5 years' experience — With BMW engines specifically, not general automotive work.
  • VAT-registered and fully insured — Public liability and professional indemnity in place.
  • Transparent pricing — No hidden charges; all quotes itemised and labour rates disclosed upfront.
  • Warranty-backed supply — Every engine supplied must carry a minimum 12-month parts and labour warranty, underwritten by the supplier (not a pass-through from a third party).
  • Customer review history — We monitor independent review platforms (Google, Trustpilot, Facebook) for sustained positive feedback. A single bad review does not disqualify, but a pattern of unresolved complaints does [EM-SURVEY].

We publish the full vetting criteria on our Specialist Vetting page, and we update the list of active specialists quarterly.

What Happens When You Enquire Through This Site

We designed the enquiry pipeline to be as transparent as possible. Below is the end-to-end journey, from the moment you submit a form to the point where your car is back on the road.

  1. You submit your enquiry — You provide your registration, engine code (if known), and describe the issue. You can also upload any diagnostic reports or photos.
  2. We match you — Our system identifies the specialists in our network who are best suited to your specific engine and location. You are matched with one specialist, not spammed by multiple.
  3. You receive a quote — The specialist contacts you directly within 2–4 hours during business hours with an itemised quote [EM-INTERVIEW]. The quote will include supply cost (used/reconditioned/rebuilt), labour, ancillaries, and warranty terms.
  4. You decide — You are under no obligation. You can compare the quote against other sources, ask questions, or decline. If you accept, the specialist arranges fitting at their workshop or a location convenient to you.
  5. Engine fitted — The specialist installs the replacement engine, runs diagnostics, and provides you with a warranty certificate and completion report.
  6. Post-sale survey — 3–6 months after your engine is fitted, we send you a short survey to gather feedback on the process, the engine performance, and the specialist's service [EM-SURVEY]. This data feeds back into our vetting and helps us improve the network.

If at any point you are unhappy, you can escalate via our Verified Outcomes process. We track every enquiry and its outcome to ensure accountability.
