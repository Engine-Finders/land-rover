What the Data Shows

The headline findings from our 2025 UK enquiry data.

Our data covers 24,650 total UK engine enquiries in 2025 across all brands [EM-VERIFIED], of which 9,000+ are Land Rover‑specific. The 204DTD (2.0 Ingenium diesel) is the most‑enquired Land Rover engine, with 1,150 enquiries, followed closely by the 306DT (3.0 TDV6/SDV6) at 980 enquiries [EM-VERIFIED].

High enquiry volumes do not necessarily mean high failure rates — they reflect vehicle population, age, and typical replacement mileage. For a proper risk assessment, we combine enquiry data with [PATTERN DATA] from workshop notes and manufacturer TSBs, and with [EM-QUOTE] cost data from actual supplier quotes.

Key findings include:

  • The 306DT crankshaft bearing failure is the single most catastrophic and expensive failure mode in our dataset, with recon costs of £6,500–£9,500 [EM-QUOTE].
  • Ingenium diesels (204DTD/AJ20D6) show timing chain tensioner wear, particularly on pre‑2019 units, with repair costs of £1,200–£2,500 for the chain alone [EM-QUOTE].
  • The 276DT (2.7 TDV6) oil pump failure is a well‑documented risk, with repair costs of £1,500–£3,000 and, if ignored, full engine replacement at £5,000–£7,500 [EM-QUOTE].
  • The 448DT (4.4 SDV8) is considered the most durable of the modern Land Rover diesels, with no catastrophic design flaw and a high repairability index [PATTERN DATA].

For a complete ranking, see the Engine Failure Index 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the data on this site come from?

Our data comes from three primary sources: (1) internal CRM enquiry data from EnginesMarket.co.uk and EngineFinders.co.uk — real quote requests from UK owners ([EM-VERIFIED]); (2) supplier quote notes and interviews with 100+ UK engine specialists ([EM-QUOTE] and [EM-INTERVIEW]); and (3) cross‑referenced with manufacturer TSBs and public forums for validation ([PATTERN DATA]). A full methodology is on the methodology page.

Are these official Land Rover or JLR data?

No. These are independently sourced data from real UK market enquiries, specialist workshops, and public sources. We are not affiliated with Jaguar Land Rover Limited or JLR Engines. All data is tagged to make its source transparent.

How can I cite this data in my own research?

Each data page includes a "How to Cite This Report" section with a suggested citation block and a permalink. You can also link directly to the methodology page for a full description of our data collection and verification processes.

Is the data updated regularly?

Yes. We update the data annually with the latest full‑year enquiry data. Interim updates are made when significant new trends emerge. Each page shows the date of the data and the date of the last update.

Can I request additional data?

Yes. If you need data that is not yet published — for example, a specific engine‑model combination or a more detailed regional breakdown — contact us and we will consider adding it to our research programme.
