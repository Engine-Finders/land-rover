Methodology

How we built this ranking from real UK enquiry data.

Source: Internal CRM quote-request data from EnginesMarket.co.uk and EngineFinders.co.uk, combined. Full calendar year 2025. 24,650 unique quote requests across all brands; 4,935 of these relate specifically to Land Rover and Range Rover engines.

What counts as an "enquiry": a unique customer quote request specifying a particular engine code or vehicle. Repeat requests from the same customer for the same engine are not double-counted.

What this data cannot tell you: our dataset reflects people who contacted us about a replacement engine — it is not a random sample of every Land Rover on UK roads. A higher enquiry count can mean an engine or model is more failure-prone, or simply that more of that vehicle exist in the parc, or that it's an older vehicle reaching typical replacement age. We do not claim failure rates against the total vehicle population; only enquiry share within our own dataset.

Refresh cycle: annually, each February, covering the prior full calendar year. Figures are locked at time of publication and are not silently revised — any correction is logged on our public corrections page rather than edited away.

What the Data Shows

The overlap between the most-enquired engines and models is striking. The 204DTD appears in the Discovery Sport, Evoque (both generations), Velar, and Defender – all high-volume modern Land Rover models. The 306DT powers the Range Rover and Range Rover Sport, which also feature high up the model ranking. This suggests that the enquiry volume is driven as much by the sheer number of these vehicles on UK roads as by any specific failure propensity. However, the presence of older models like the L538 Evoque and L320 Sport (with the 276DT and 368DT) indicates that age and mileage also play a role.

It's also worth noting that the 204DTD (Ingenium diesel) is the most-enquired engine across all brands in our full 2025 dataset (1,150), outstripping even the BMW N47 (1,450) when you consider that the N47 also appears in many non-Land Rover models. This underscores the significance of Land Rover's Ingenium diesel in the UK replacement market.

What This Means For Owners

If you own a Discovery Sport, Evoque, or Range Rover Sport, you're driving one of the most common vehicles we see in our enquiry data. That doesn't mean your car is destined to fail – it means that if you do need a replacement engine, there is a well-established supply chain and a network of specialists familiar with these models. The 204DTD, for example, is widely available in reconditioned form, and many independent specialists have deep experience with its timing chain and oil dilution issues.

For older models like the L538 Evoque and L320 Sport, the repair-or-replace decision is more nuanced. The 224DT (Ford-sourced) is cheaper to replace, but the 276DT and 368DT come with higher costs and known failure modes – see our 276DT oil pump failure and 368DT turbo/injector pages for detailed guidance. Frequently Asked Questions

Which Land Rover model generates the most engine replacement enquiries?

The Discovery Sport L550 leads with 362 enquiries in our 2025 data, closely followed by the Evoque L551 at 349. This reflects the popularity of these compact SUVs in the UK market.

Which Land Rover engine is most often requested for replacement?

The 204DTD Ingenium diesel tops the list with 1,150 enquiries, more than double the next engine (306DT at 980). It is fitted to many modern Land Rover models.

Does a high enquiry count mean the engine is unreliable?

Not necessarily. Enquiry volume is influenced by vehicle parc size and age. We combine enquiry data with our Known Failure Patterns research to give a fuller picture.

How often is this data updated?

Annually, each February, covering the prior full calendar year. This is our first published ranking, and future editions will show year-on-year changes.
