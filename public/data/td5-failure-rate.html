Common failures are not catastrophic — they include injector loom oil contamination, fuel pressure regulator seals, and occasional head gasket issues — with repair costs typically ranging from £200 to £1,200 [ESTIMATED].

Methodology

How this data was assembled and what it can tell you.

The TD5 does not appear in our 2025 engine enquiry dataset, which focuses on engines that typically need replacement — the TD5 is more often repaired than replaced, which explains its absence. To build a useful picture of TD5 reliability, we have drawn from:

  • Specialist interviews – conversations with Land Rover workshops that regularly maintain TD5‑powered Defenders and Discoveries [EM-INTERVIEW]
  • Owner forums and reports – patterns observed across multiple high‑mileage examples [PATTERN DATA]
  • Our own failure patterns table – though the TD5 is not listed there, we cross‑reference similar age engines for cost estimates [ESTIMATED]

Where we present figures, they are clearly tagged. This is not a statistically controlled survey, but a consolidated view from the sources our network trusts.

Failure Rate in Context

The TD5 is often held up as one of the most reliable Land Rover engines ever made. While we cannot compare it directly to our 2025 enquiry data (since it is not in the list), its absence from a list dominated by 204DTD and 306DT is telling. Those engines appear because they are reaching replacement age in large numbers. The TD5, by contrast, tends to be kept on the road through maintenance rather than replacement.

For context, our 2025 data shows:

  • The most‑enquired engine was the 204DTD with 1,150 enquiries [EM-VERIFIED]
  • The 306DT followed with 980 enquiries [EM-VERIFIED]
  • Even the 224DT (2.2 Duratorq) had 350 enquiries [EM-VERIFIED]

The TD5 is not in that table, which is entirely consistent with its reputation for durability when properly maintained. That does not mean it never fails – but when it does, the failure is typically repairable without replacing the whole engine.

How Long They Actually Last

A well‑maintained TD5 can easily exceed 200,000 miles, and many examples are still running strong beyond 250,000 [PATTERN DATA]. The key factors that determine longevity are:

  • Regular oil changes – every 6,000 miles or annually, using the correct 5W‑40 specification [ESTIMATED]
  • Coolant maintenance – replacing coolant every 3–4 years to prevent head gasket stress
  • Injector harness inspection – replacing the harness as a preventative measure at around 80,000 miles can avoid oil‑related damage to the ECU
  • Fuel system care – using clean fuel and changing the fuel filter regularly

Most specialists agree that the TD5 is not prone to catastrophic failure; it gives plenty of warning before a major issue occurs, and repairs are almost always cheaper than a full engine replacement. This is reflected in the fact that we rarely see TD5 replacement enquiries – owners fix them.

Our Verdict

Reliability Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)

The TD5 is one of the most dependable engines Land Rover has produced. It is not without its foibles – the injector loom and head gasket are known weak points – but these are manageable and predictable. With proper care, a TD5 will outlast many of the vehicles it is fitted to. It is a better choice for long‑term ownership than the later, more complex Ingenium diesels, and it remains a favourite among independent specialists. [PATTERN DATA]

If you own a TD5‑powered Defender or Discovery 2, our advice is to stay on top of the loom and cooling system. Replacement engines are not cheap – used TD5 units typically cost £1,500–£3,000 supply‑only [ESTIMATED] – but you are unlikely to need one if you follow the preventative measures above.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the TD5 a reliable engine?

Yes, it is widely considered one of the most reliable Land Rover diesels. With proper maintenance, it regularly exceeds 200,000 miles. Its weak points are well understood and repairable.

What is the most common TD5 failure?

Injector harness oil contamination is the most frequent issue, causing misfires and poor running. It is a relatively inexpensive fix (£200–£400).

At what mileage does a TD5 need major work?

Head gasket and turbo issues tend to appear between 120,000 and 150,000 miles. Many TD5s go beyond that without major failure if the cooling system is well maintained.

Is a TD5 engine replacement expensive?

Used TD5 engines are available from £1,500–£3,000 supply‑only; a reconditioned unit may cost £3,000–£5,000 fitted [ESTIMATED]. However, most owners repair rather than replace.

How can I extend the life of my TD5?

Regular oil changes (6,000 miles), coolant flushes every 3 years, and replacing the injector harness proactively at 80,000 miles will significantly extend engine life.
