Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average Land Rover engine replacement cost?

The average varies widely by model – from £800–£1,800 for a used 224DT to £8,000–£12,000 for a reconditioned 508PS SDV8. See our Average Replacement Cost by Model page for the full breakdown.

What is the 60% scrap threshold?

The 60% threshold is a rule of thumb: if the lowest viable replacement cost exceeds approximately 60% of the vehicle's current market value, consider scrapping. See the Repair, Replace or Scrap Framework for worked examples.

Are these economics pages based on real data?

Yes. Every page is backed by real enquiry volumes, supplier quotes, and post‑sale surveys. We do not publish anecdotal or speculative information.

How do I find the right economics page for my decision?

Use the categories above or start with our Repair vs Replace Calculator to run your own numbers. You can also search for your model or engine code.

Can I get a quote from a specialist through this site?

How often is the economics centre updated?

We update pages quarterly with new enquiry data and specialist feedback. Major updates are announced on our Data page.
