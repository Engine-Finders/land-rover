The short answer: a 306DT failure is often a write‑off if your vehicle is worth less than about £11,000–£16,000, depending on the exact repair cost and whether you choose used or reconditioned. The engine is notorious for catastrophic crankshaft bearing failure, and our 2025 data shows that repair costs frequently exceed 60% of the vehicle's market value on older, higher‑mileage examples.

  • The 306DT generated 980 quote requests in 2025 – the most of any Land Rover diesel [EM‑VERIFIED]
  • A reconditioned 306DT supply‑and‑fit typically costs £6,500–£9,500 [EM‑QUOTE]; a used unit is cheaper at £3,500–£5,500 but carries higher risk

  • For a Discovery 4 worth £9,000, a recon at £7,800 gives a ratio of 87% – clearly a write‑off. For a low‑mileage L405 worth £30,000, the same repair is only 26% – safe

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a 306DT engine failure and why is it so expensive?

The 306DT (3.0 TDV6/SDV6) suffers from crankshaft bearing failure – the tin‑aluminium coating on the bearings fatigues, leading to catastrophic seizure. Repair requires a full bottom‑end rebuild or a replacement engine, with labour intensive due to the engine's position. Typical reconditioned supply‑and‑fit costs range from £6,500 to £9,500 [EM-QUOTE].

How do I know if my 306DT failure is a write‑off?

Apply the 60% rule: divide your repair quote by the current trade value of your vehicle. If the result is above 0.6, it's a write‑off. For example, a £7,800 quote on a £9,000 car gives 87% – scrap. Use our calculator to run your own numbers.

Can I use a used 306DT engine to make it cheaper?

A used unit is cheaper (£3,500–£5,500 supply‑only) but comes with no warranty and unknown history. The 306DT is notorious for bearing failure, so a used engine may fail again soon. A reconditioned unit costs 30–45% more but includes new bearings, oil pump and a warranty – we generally recommend it if you're keeping the car. See our guide.

Which models are most affected by 306DT failure?

All models with the 306DT are affected – Discovery 4, Range Rover Sport (L494 and L320), Range Rover L405, Discovery 5 and Velar D300. Our enquiry data shows the highest volume comes from Discovery 4 owners, reflecting both the age of the vehicle and the high failure rate at around 80k–140k miles [PATTERN DATA].

Is it ever worth repairing a 306DT on a Discovery 4?

Yes – if the vehicle is in exceptional condition, has low mileage, or is a rare specification (e.g., HSE Luxury with full service history). In those cases, the car may be worth more than the average trade value, and the repair may clear the 60% threshold. Always get a professional valuation before deciding.

Does the 60% rule apply to the 306DT in a Range Rover Sport L494?

Yes, the same rule applies. L494 values are generally higher than Discovery 4, so a 306DT failure is less likely to be a write‑off on an L494. For example, a £15,000 L494 with a £7,800 quote gives a ratio of 52% – safe. However, older, higher‑mileage L494s may still fall into the watch zone.
