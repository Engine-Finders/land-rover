The short answer: it depends entirely on which Discovery you own, which engine is fitted, and the current market value of the vehicle. A 2018 Discovery 5 with a failed 204DTD Ingenium diesel is almost always worth repairing; a 2007 Discovery 3 with a snapped 276DT crankshaft is often a financial write-off.

  • Our proprietary 2025 UK enquiry data shows that Discovery models make up nearly 20% of all Land Rover engine replacement requests [EM-VERIFIED]
  • The most frequently enquired Discovery engine is the 306DT (SDV6/TDV6), with 980 requests in 2025 [EM-VERIFIED] – and a reconditioned supply cost of £6,500–£9,500 [EM-QUOTE]
  • The 204DTD Ingenium diesel comes next with 1,150 requests across all models (including Discovery Sport) and a recon cost of £3,500–£5,800 [EM-QUOTE]
  • We apply a 60% repair-to-value threshold – if the repair costs more than 60% of the car's current trade value, the honest answer is usually to scrap or sell for parts

When the Honest Answer Is No

Sometimes the best financial decision is to walk away. These are the Discovery scenarios where our data suggests scrapping or parting out is the better choice.

🚫

High‑Mileage Discovery 3 with 276DT Failure

With typical trade values below £5,000, a 276DT recon at £5,000–£7,500 frequently exceeds 80% of the car's value. Add in corroded chassis and worn suspension, and the honest verdict is usually to scrap. See our Discovery economics for full detail.

🚫

Discovery 4 SDV6 with Sub‑£8,000 Value

The 306DT crankshaft failure is catastrophic and expensive. If your Discovery 4 is worth less than £8,000, a recon at £7,500–£9,500 will almost certainly exceed the 60% threshold. We cover this in detail in our Discovery SDV6 case study.

Any Discovery with Multiple Secondary Faults

If your Discovery also needs a gearbox, air suspension or a new infotainment system, the cumulative repair cost can push the total well beyond the value of the vehicle – even if the engine alone would have cleared the threshold. Use our Repair vs Replace Calculator to factor in all costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it ever worth replacing a Discovery 3 engine?

Yes – but only if the vehicle is in exceptional condition and the engine failure is a known single-point fault (e.g., oil pump housing on the 276DT). For most Discovery 3s with trade values under £5,000, the answer is no. Check our 276DT Knowledge Centre for specific guidance.

What is the most reliable Discovery engine?

Based on our 2025 enquiry data, the Td5 and 204DT Ingenium petrol have the lowest failure‑related request volumes relative to their vehicle parc. However, no Discovery engine is immune – the full reliability picture is in our Engine Failure Index 2026.

How much does a Discovery 5 engine replacement cost?

For a 204DTD diesel, reconditioned supply and fit typically costs £3,500–£5,800 [EM-QUOTE]. For the 306DT (fitted to early Discovery 5 models), expect £6,500–£9,500. Labour adds 10–14 hours for the 204DTD and 15–20 hours for the 306DT at £65–£85/hr indie rates.

Should I buy a used Discovery engine or reconditioned?

A used unit is cheaper (£1,500–£4,500) but comes with no warranty and unknown history. A reconditioned engine costs 30–45% more but includes a rebuild warranty and new wear parts. Our Reconditioned vs Used vs Rebuilt guide explains the trade‑offs.

Can I replace a Discovery 4 engine with a Discovery 5 engine?

No – the engine mounts, ECU, wiring harness and emissions systems are not interchangeable. Always use the exact engine code specified for your VIN. Use our Engine Compatibility Checker to confirm fitment.

Does the 60% rule apply to Discovery Sport as well?

Yes – the same threshold applies. Discovery Sport values are generally higher than Discovery 3/4, so the verdict often leans towards repair. For model‑specific advice, see Is It Worth Replacing a Discovery Sport Engine?
