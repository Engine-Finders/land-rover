When the Most Expensive Repair Is a Write‑Off

Some repairs are so costly relative to the vehicle's value that the only sensible answer is to scrap or sell for parts. Here are the cases where our data shows owners walk away – and they're right to.

🚫

306DT on a Sub‑£10k Discovery 4

With recon costs at £6,500–£9,500, a Discovery 4 worth £9,000 gives a ratio of 72–105% – well above the 60% threshold. Our 306DT write‑off guide covers this in detail.

🚫

276DT on a Sub‑£7k Discovery 3

Recon costs £5,000–£7,500 on a vehicle worth £6,000 – that's 83–125%. The oil pump failure is catastrophic, and the vehicle's age rarely justifies the spend. See our 276DT oil pump failure page.

🚫

368DT on an Early L322 or L320

Pre‑2010 3.6 TDV8 units have crankshaft fracture risks. With recon at £5,500–£8,000 and typical L322 values under £10,000, this repair often exceeds 60%. Our 368DT Knowledge Centre warns against early examples.

🚫

224DT on a Freelander 2

Though cheaper at £2,000–£3,500, a Freelander 2 often sits under £4,000 – making the ratio 50–87%. Combined with other age‑related issues, the honest answer is frequently to scrap. Our Freelander economics explains.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the single most expensive Land Rover engine repair?

The 508PS SDV8 (4.4 V8) has the highest reconditioned supply‑and‑fit cost at £8,000–£12,000 [EM-QUOTE]. However, the 306DT crankshaft failure is the most commonly quoted catastrophic repair, with 980 enquiries in 2025 [EM-VERIFIED].

How does the 60% rule apply to expensive repairs?

We divide the repair cost by the vehicle's current trade value. If the result exceeds 60%, we recommend scrapping or selling for parts. For example, a £7,800 repair on a £9,000 Discovery 4 is 87% – a clear write‑off. Full methodology on our framework page.

Are used engines a cheaper alternative for expensive repairs?

Yes – a used unit can save 30–50% but carries higher risk. For the 306DT, a used engine costs £3,500–£5,500 versus £6,500–£9,500 recon. However, the 306DT's bearing failure can recur if the used engine is not properly inspected. Our guide explains the trade‑offs.

Which Land Rover model has the most expensive repair costs on average?

Range Rover L405 and L494 models with the 508PS or 306DT engines top the list. However, the average repair cost is lower than the peak – most owners spend £3,000–£6,000. See our average cost by model for full details.
