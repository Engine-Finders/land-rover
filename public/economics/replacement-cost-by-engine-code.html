Understanding the Cost Columns

What do these numbers actually mean for your wallet? Here’s how to interpret each cost type and which to choose.

🟢

Used (Supply‑Only)

The cheapest option – a used engine pulled from another vehicle. Prices range from £800 for a 2.2 Duratorq to over £7,000 for a flagship V8. Risk: unknown history, limited warranty (usually 30–90 days). Best for low‑value vehicles or short‑term ownership.

🟡

Reconditioned (Supply‑Only)

The most popular choice – 70.45% of our 2025 enquiries were for reconditioned units [EM-VERIFIED]. These engines are stripped, cleaned, inspected and have worn parts replaced to a standard specification. Warranty: typically 12–24 months. Balances cost and reliability.

🔴

Rebuilt (Supply‑Only)

The highest‑cost option – a full strip, machine work (boring, grinding) and reassembly with new components, often with upgrades. Costs can be 50–80% above reconditioned. Best for: long‑term keepers, classics, or high‑performance builds where OEM specification is critical.

For a detailed comparison of these three options, see our full guide: Reconditioned vs Used vs Rebuilt Engines.

How to Use This Cost Data

Turning a cost range into a decision requires applying the 60% repair‑to‑value rule. Here’s how.

  1. Find your engine code – use the table above to get the supply‑only range for the option you’re considering (used, recon, or rebuilt).
  2. Add labour and ancillaries – add the fitted premium from the labour table, plus an extra £200–£500 for gaskets, fluids, filters and sundries.
  3. Estimate your vehicle’s current trade value – use online valuation tools or a local dealer. Be realistic.
  4. Calculate the repair‑to‑value ratio – divide your total fitted cost by the vehicle value. If the result is below 60%, replacement is likely financially sensible. Above 60%, consider scrapping or selling as a non‑runner.

Our Verdict: Which Engine Is Cheapest to Replace?

Based on our 2025 data, the cheapest Land Rover engine to replace is the 224DT (2.2 Duratorq), with reconditioned supply costs starting around £2,000. The most expensive are the V8 diesels – the 508PS and 448DT – with reconditioned supply costs exceeding £6,000–£8,000.

However, cost alone doesn’t decide. The 224DT is found in older Freelanders and Evoques, where vehicle values may not justify a recon unit – many owners choose used engines in those cases. The V8 diesels are fitted to high‑value Range Rovers, where the replacement cost is often well within the 60% threshold.

Always apply the 60% rule with your own vehicle’s value. The numbers above give you the raw cost; your vehicle’s value and your ownership horizon determine whether replacement is the right answer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does “supply‑only” mean?

Supply‑only means the cost of the engine unit itself, without fitting. You will need to add labour, fluids, gaskets and other ancillaries to get the final fitted price.

Why are reconditioned engines more expensive than used?

A reconditioned engine has been fully stripped, cleaned, inspected and has worn parts replaced – it’s essentially a “new” engine with new bearings, rings, seals and sometimes timing components. A used engine is sold “as is” with no guarantee of internal condition.

How do I get an accurate quote for my engine?

The best way is to contact our vetted specialist network through the quote comparison tool. Provide your vehicle registration, engine code and mileage, and you’ll receive supply‑and‑fit quotes from multiple specialists.

Are main dealer costs included in these figures?

No – these figures are for independent specialists. Main dealer labour rates can be 30–50% higher, and their parts pricing may also be above independent supply. Always compare both.

Does the cost table include all Land Rover engines?

It includes the most common modern Land Rover engines for which we have robust 2025 quote data. Older engines (e.g., TD5, 300TDI) are not listed because they are rarely replaced with a recon unit – they are often rebuilt in‑house. For those, please contact a specialist for a custom quote.
