Frequently Asked Questions

How many Land Rover engines are covered in this database?

We currently list 30+ engines that have appeared in our 2025 UK enquiry data. This includes classic diesels (200TDi, 300TDi, TD5), V6/V8 diesels (TDV6, SDV6, TDV8, SDV8), the Ingenium family, and various petrol V8s and four‑cylinder units.

Are these engines listed by model or by engine code?

They are listed by engine code (e.g. 306DT, 204DTD), which is the most precise way to identify a Land Rover engine. Each engine page also shows all models and years it was fitted to, so you can cross‑reference by vehicle.

Where does the data on each engine page come from?

Every number is tagged with its source — [EM-VERIFIED] for enquiry volumes, [EM-QUOTE] for cost data, [PATTERN DATA] for expert‑level failure patterns, and [THIRD-PARTY] for publicly sourced information. A full methodology is on our data methodology page.

How can I tell which engine I have in my vehicle?

Use our registration lookup tool (enter your number plate) or check the engine number stamp on the block — we have a guide to engine number locations for each model.
