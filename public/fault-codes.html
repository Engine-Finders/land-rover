Engine Timing & Powertrain Codes

Codes related to camshaft, crankshaft, timing, and general engine performance.

P0011 — Camshaft Position Timing Over‑Advanced (Bank 1)

VVT system malfunction – often a stuck solenoid, low oil pressure, or timing chain stretch.

P0016 — Crankshaft / Camshaft Position Correlation

Physical timing offset – almost always chain stretch or tensioner failure.

P0300 — Random / Multiple Cylinder Misfire

Random misfire – causes include ignition, fuel, or timing issues.

P0303 — Cylinder 3 Misfire

Specific cylinder misfire – injector, coil, or compression fault.

P0217 — Engine Over‑Temperature Condition

Overheating – cooling system, water pump, or head gasket issues.

P0234 — Turbocharger Overboost

Turbo overboost – wastegate, actuator, or boost pressure sensor fault.

P0299 — Turbocharger Underboost

Low boost – boost leak, turbo failure, or actuator issues.

P0263 — Cylinder 1 Contribution / Balance Fault

Uneven cylinder contribution – injector or compression imbalance.

P0128 — Coolant Thermostat (Coolant Temperature Below Thermostat Regulating Temperature)

Thermostat stuck open – engine takes too long to warm up.

Fuel & Air System Codes

Codes related to fuel pressure, fuel delivery, and air management.

P0087 — Fuel Rail Pressure Too Low

Low fuel pressure – fuel pump, pressure regulator, or filter issues.

P0088 — Fuel Rail Pressure Too High

High fuel pressure – pressure regulator or return line restricted.

P0090 — Fuel Pressure Regulator 1 Control Circuit

Fuel pressure regulator circuit – wiring or solenoid fault.

P0193 — Fuel Rail Pressure Sensor Circuit High Input

Fuel rail pressure sensor signal too high – sensor or wiring fault.

P0401 — EGR Flow Insufficient Detected

EGR valve stuck or clogged – common on high‑mileage diesels.

P0402 — EGR Flow Excessive Detected

EGR valve stuck open – causes rough idle and poor performance.

P0403 — EGR Control Circuit

EGR circuit electrical fault – wiring, connector, or solenoid issue.

P2002 — DPF Efficiency Below Threshold (Bank 1)

DPF clogged or cracked – regeneration failure or ash build‑up.

P204F — Reductant System Performance (Bank 1)

AdBlue / SCR system performance – pump, injector, or NOx sensor issue.

P20E8 — Reductant Pressure Too Low

AdBlue pressure low – pump failure or line blockage.

P244A — DPF Differential Pressure Too Low

DPF differential pressure sensor reading low – sensor fault or wiring.

P244B — DPF Differential Pressure Too High

DPF differential pressure too high – clogged DPF or sensor fault.

P2463 — DPF Soot Accumulation

Excessive soot in DPF – regeneration needed or failed.

P246C — DPF Regeneration Incomplete

DPF regeneration interrupted – typically caused by short trips.

For DPF‑related symptoms, see our DPF warning light and DPF blockage failure guides.

Emission & Exhaust Codes

Codes related to catalytic converter, oxygen sensors, and evaporative systems.

P0420 — Catalyst System Efficiency Below Threshold (Bank 1)

Catalytic converter underperforming – contaminated or failed.

P0670 — Glow Plug Module Control Circuit

Glow plug control circuit – module or wiring fault.

P164B — Oil Pressure Control Solenoid – Circuit Open / Short

Oil pressure solenoid circuit fault – affects VVT and timing control.

P132B — Turbocharger / Supercharger Boost Control Performance

Boost control performance – wastegate or actuator not responding correctly.

For related warning lights, see our warning lights index.

Electrical, Network & General Codes

Codes related to battery, voltage, CAN bus, and other electrical systems.

P0562 — System Voltage Low

Low battery voltage – alternator, battery, or wiring issue.

P0700 — Transmission Control System (MIL Request)

Generic transmission fault – often accompanied by a specific transmission code.

U0100 — Lost Communication with ECM / PCM

Loss of communication with engine control module – CAN bus or module fault.
