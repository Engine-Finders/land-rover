Ingenium Petrol vs Ingenium Diesel — Which Land Rover Ingenium Do I Have?

The Land Rover Ingenium engine family includes both petrol and diesel variants across 2.0-litre and 3.0-litre configurations. This guide shows you exactly how to tell them apart, how to identify your engine code, and what to check when buying a replacement.

The Short Answer

You can tell petrol and diesel Ingenium engines apart by checking the engine code label, the fuel filler cap, and the 8th character of your VIN. The 2.0-litre petrol Ingenium is coded 204PT (or AJ200P), while the 2.0-litre diesel is 204DTD (or AJ200D). The 3.0-litre petrol is 306PS (AJ20P6), and the 3.0-litre diesel is AJ20D6. Diesel engines also have a fuel filler neck designed for AdBlue nozzles.

  • Petrol Ingenium codes: 204PT (2.0), 306PS (3.0) – more reliable, lower replacement costs.
  • Diesel Ingenium codes: 204DTD (2.0), AJ20D6 (3.0) – higher enquiry volume but more failure-prone.
  • Engine requests in 2025: 1,150 for 204DTD diesel [EM-VERIFIED] vs 480 for 204PT petrol [EM-VERIFIED].

How to Confirm Which Ingenium Engine Is in Your Land Rover

There are four reliable ways to confirm whether your Land Rover has a petrol or diesel Ingenium engine. Use the method that best suits your situation.

  1. Check the engine code label.

    Open the bonnet and locate the engine code label on the timing cover or cylinder head. The code will be clearly stamped as 204PT (petrol 2.0), 204DTD (diesel 2.0), 306PS (petrol 3.0), or AJ20D6 (diesel 3.0). This is the most direct and reliable method.

  2. Use the Land Rover VIN decoder.

    Your vehicle's VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) can be decoded to reveal the engine type. The 8th character of the VIN indicates the engine. For the 204PT petrol, the 8th character will typically be D (200 PS), E (250 PS), or F (300 PS). For the 204DTD diesel, it will be a different letter. You can verify this using a free online Land Rover VIN decoder.

  3. Check the fuel filler cap and neck.

    Diesel Ingenium engines have a larger fuel filler neck designed for AdBlue nozzles (blue ring around the neck). Petrol engines have a standard unleaded filler neck. This is a quick visual check that doesn't require opening the bonnet.

  4. Check your Land Rover's registration document.

    The V5C logbook includes the engine number and fuel type. While this is less direct than the other methods, it is a useful secondary check if the engine label is not accessible.

Why Correct Engine Identification Matters

Getting the engine code right is critical — especially if you are considering a replacement. Installing the wrong engine variant can lead to:

  • ECU compatibility issues — Petrol and diesel ECUs are completely different; a mismatch will cause running problems or immobilisation.
  • Fuel system incompatibility — Diesel engines have high-pressure common-rail injection systems, petrol engines have direct injection with spark plugs. They are not interchangeable.
  • Exhaust and emissions differences — Diesel Ingenium engines have DPFs, AdBlue/SCR systems, and different exhaust layouts. Installing a petrol engine in a diesel-chassis (or vice versa) will create MOT and emissions test failures.
  • Voided warranty or specialist support — Most vetted Land Rover specialists will not work on mismatched engine/chassis combinations.

Always check the Engine Code Guide and consult a specialist before purchasing a used or reconditioned engine. The Ingenium petrol (204PT) is generally considered more reliable than the diesel (204DTD), but both are complex engines that require specialist knowledge for replacement.

Common Misidentifications: What Your Ingenium Is Not

The Ingenium engine family is frequently confused with other Land Rover engines. Here's what to look out for to avoid a costly mistake.

204DTD (2.0 Diesel) vs 204PT (2.0 Petrol)

These share the same 2.0-litre displacement and basic architecture but are completely different engines with different fuel systems, compression ratios, and ECUs. The diesel has a DPF and AdBlue system; the petrol does not. The engine code is the only reliable way to tell them apart.

AJ20D6 (3.0 Diesel) vs 306PS (3.0 Petrol)

Both are 3.0-litre straight-six Ingenium engines, but one is diesel and one is petrol. The diesel (AJ20D6) has DPF and AdBlue; the petrol (306PS) does not. They have different engine codes, fuel filler necks, and ECU mappings. Do not confuse them.

Old vs New Ingenium Revisions

Early Ingenium engines (pre-2019) have known timing chain tensioner issues (especially on diesel) and cooling system problems (on petrol). Later revisions (2019+) are more robust. When buying a replacement, the newer revision is preferred. Always check the production date of the donor engine.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I tell if my Land Rover Ingenium is petrol or diesel?

The most reliable way is to check the engine code label on the timing cover. 204PT = 2.0 petrol, 204DTD = 2.0 diesel, 306PS = 3.0 petrol, AJ20D6 = 3.0 diesel. You can also check the fuel filler neck (diesel has a blue AdBlue ring) or the 8th character of your VIN.

What is the difference between 204PT and 204DTD?

204PT is the 2.0-litre petrol Ingenium. 204DTD is the 2.0-litre diesel Ingenium. They share the same basic architecture but have different fuel systems, compression ratios, and emission control equipment. The diesel has a DPF and AdBlue system; the petrol does not.

Is the petrol Ingenium more reliable than the diesel?

Generally, yes. The petrol Ingenium (204PT, 306PS) is considered more reliable than the diesel (204DTD, AJ20D6). The diesel has known timing chain tensioner issues (pre-2019), oil dilution problems from DPF regeneration, and EGR cooler failures. The petrol's main weakness is the water pump and cooling system.

Can I fit a petrol Ingenium engine into a diesel-chassis Land Rover?

Not directly. Petrol and diesel Ingenium engines have different engine mounts, fuel systems, ECU systems, exhaust layouts, and wiring looms. A conversion is technically possible but prohibitively expensive and rarely worthwhile. Always replace like-for-like.

How much does a replacement Ingenium engine cost?

Based on our internal quote data [EM-QUOTE], a reconditioned 204PT petrol engine supply-only ranges from £3,000 to £5,000. A reconditioned 204DTD diesel engine ranges from £3,500 to £5,800. Fitting costs (R&R labour) at an independent specialist are typically £650–£910. A used engine may be cheaper, but condition is key.

What are the engine codes for Land Rover Ingenium engines?

2.0 petrol: 204PT (AJ200P). 2.0 diesel: 204DTD (AJ200D). 3.0 petrol: 306PS (AJ20P6). 3.0 diesel: AJ20D6 (AJ300). You can find the code on a label on the timing cover, or by decoding the VIN.

Our Verdict: Petrol or Diesel — Which Ingenium Is Right for Your Land Rover?

⚠️ Watch

The choice between petrol and diesel Ingenium depends on your usage and tolerance for risk. The petrol Ingenium (204PT, 306PS) is generally more reliable, with lower running costs and fewer catastrophic failure modes. The diesel Ingenium (204DTD, AJ20D6) offers better fuel economy and torque for towing, but has known timing chain, oil dilution, and EGR issues that can be expensive to repair.

If your Ingenium has failed and the cost of replacement exceeds approximately 60% of your Land Rover's current market value, you may need to consider whether replacement is financially viable. However, given the high retained values of modern Land Rovers, a reconditioned engine is often a sensible investment — especially if you choose the petrol variant.
