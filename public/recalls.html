How to Check Your VIN for Recalls

The quickest way to check if your vehicle has an outstanding recall.

Step 1 — Find your VIN. Your 17‑digit VIN is visible through the windscreen on the driver's side, on the door jamb sticker, or in your V5C logbook.

Step 2 — Visit the DVSA recall checker. The official UK government recall checker allows you to enter your VIN and see any outstanding safety recalls. We link to it from each model page.

Step 3 — Check our model pages. Our recall pages list all known recalls for each model, including the affected VIN ranges — so you can see if your vehicle falls within a recall campaign even before you run the official check.

Step 4 — Contact a specialist. If your vehicle has an outstanding recall, your nearest JLR dealership will carry out the repair free of charge. For non‑recall but known faults, our Failure Pages and Diagnostic Wizard can help.

What's Covered by a Recall — and What Isn't

A safety recall is issued when a manufacturer identifies a defect that could affect the safety of the vehicle. Recall remedies are always free of charge at authorised dealerships.

✔️ Covered by recall: factory‑identified safety defects, such as EGR cooler fires, timing chain tensioner failures that could cause sudden loss of power, fuel system leaks, and airbag or electrical issues that affect safety.

❌ Not covered by recall: general wear and tear, age‑related failures, or known faults that are not safety‑critical. For example, a timing chain tensioner failure that occurs after 100,000 miles on an engine with a known design weakness may be a known fault but not a recall — the difference is critical when deciding whether the repair is free or chargeable.

Our model recall pages clearly differentiate between safety recalls (free) and known faults (chargeable). For known faults, our Failure Pages give you the repair cost and decision framework.

If your vehicle has a recall but you've already paid for the repair, you may be eligible for reimbursement — check the recall notice or speak to your dealer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if my Land Rover has a recall?

Use our model recall pages to see all known recalls for your model, or enter your 17‑digit VIN on the official DVSA recall checker. We link to the DVSA checker from each model page.

Are recall repairs free?

Yes — all safety recall repairs are carried out free of charge at authorised JLR dealerships. If you've already paid for a repair that later becomes a recall, you may be eligible for reimbursement.

What's the difference between a recall and a known fault?

A recall is a safety‑critical defect identified by the manufacturer, with a free remedy. A known fault is a common issue that is not safety‑critical — repairs are chargeable. Our model pages list both, clearly labelled.

Can I check my VIN without using the official DVSA site?

Yes — our model recall pages list the affected VIN ranges for each recall campaign. If your VIN falls within the range, your vehicle is affected. For the official confirmation, always check the DVSA or your dealer.

What if my model isn't listed?

We cover all current Land Rover models plus the most common older models. If your model isn't listed, use the registration lookup to confirm your vehicle details, or contact us and we will add it.
