The Short Answer

White smoke from a TD5 engine is usually caused by coolant leaking into the combustion chamber (head gasket failure), or unburned diesel from a faulty injector. A sweet smell indicates coolant; a raw diesel smell points to injector issues. Repair costs range from £200–£500 for injector work to £1,500–£3,000 for a head gasket, and up to £4,000–£7,500 for a replacement engine if the cylinder head is cracked. Condensation on cold start is normal and disappears within a minute.

What the TD5 Specifically Does

White smoke from a TD5 can range from a harmless puff on a cold morning to a thick cloud that signals imminent engine failure. Here is what to look for.

💨 White smoke on cold start (disappears)

A small puff of white smoke that vanishes within 30–60 seconds on a cold morning is often just condensation in the exhaust. This is normal and not a concern.

💨 Thick white smoke that lingers

Continuous white smoke, especially when the engine is warm, suggests coolant entering the combustion chamber – usually a blown head gasket or a cracked cylinder head. A sweet smell is a telltale sign.

💨 White smoke with a diesel smell

If the smoke smells like raw diesel, it's likely unburned fuel from a faulty injector – often a leaking or poor-spraying injector that dumps too much diesel.

What else to look for: White smoke is often accompanied by coolant loss (without a visible leak), a engine management light, overheating, or a oil-in-coolant mayonnaise. See our guides on TD5 blue smoke and TD5 black smoke for other exhaust colours.

Why This Engine In Particular — The TD5's Known Weaknesses

The TD5 is a robust engine, but it has a few well-known failure points that can cause white smoke. Understanding these helps you and your specialist pinpoint the issue quickly.

🔧 Head Gasket Failure (Most Common)

The TD5's head gasket can fail, especially if the engine has ever overheated. When it blows, coolant can leak into the cylinders, producing thick white smoke with a sweet smell. This is a serious issue that requires immediate attention – driving with a blown head gasket can warp the cylinder head.

🔧 Faulty Injectors (Over‑fuelling)

Worn or leaking injectors can cause excessive diesel to be injected into the cylinders. This unburned fuel exits as white smoke with a strong diesel smell. This is more common on high‑mileage engines and can also cause black smoke under load.

🔧 Cracked Cylinder Head (Rare)

In severe cases, especially after repeated overheating, the cylinder head can crack. This allows coolant to leak directly into the combustion chamber, producing persistent white smoke. Repairing this often requires a replacement head or a reconditioned engine.

How a Specialist Confirms the Cause

Diagnosing white smoke on a TD5 requires a systematic approach to differentiate between head gasket failure, injector issues, and a cracked head.

1

Cooling System Pressure Test

They'll pressurise the cooling system and check for leaks. If pressure drops and there's no external leak, coolant is likely entering the combustion chamber.

2

Hydrocarbon (Block) Test

A chemical test of the coolant for combustion gases – a positive result confirms a head gasket breach or a crack.

3

Injector Leak-Off Test

Measuring the amount of diesel returning from each injector can identify a worn injector that is over‑fuelling and causing white smoke.

4

Cylinder Compression Test

Low compression in one or more cylinders can point to a head gasket failure or worn piston rings.

5

Visual Inspection of Oil and Coolant

Checking for oil in the coolant (mayonnaise) or coolant in the oil (milky oil) provides strong clues about a head gasket issue.

Frequently Asked Questions

What causes a TD5 to blow white smoke?

The most common causes are a failed head gasket (coolant in cylinders), faulty injectors (unburned diesel), or a cracked cylinder head. Condensation on cold starts is normal and clears quickly.

How much does it cost to fix white smoke on a TD5?

Costs range from £300–£600 for injector work, £1,500–£3,000 for a head gasket, £2,500–£4,500 for a cylinder head, and up to £4,000–£7,500 for a replacement engine.

Can I drive with a TD5 that blows white smoke?

If it's a small puff on cold start, you can drive. However, persistent white smoke indicates a serious problem – do not drive, as continuing can cause major engine damage.

Is white smoke from a TD5 always the head gasket?

No. It can also be caused by faulty injectors or a cracked head. A proper diagnosis with a coolant pressure test and hydrocarbon test will confirm.

What should I do if my TD5 is blowing white smoke?

Check the coolant level and look for a sweet smell. If the smoke is thick or accompanied by coolant loss, call a specialist immediately. Book a diagnostic test.

Is white smoke covered by a warranty?

It depends. Manufacturing defects may be covered, but wear‑and‑tear items like injectors and head gaskets are usually excluded. Damage from overheating is often not covered.
