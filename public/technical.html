Frequently Asked Questions

Is the technical data on this site official Land Rover data?

No – it is independently sourced and cross‑verified. We combine manufacturer published data with real‑world specialist knowledge and our own enquiry data to produce a reference that is more practical than a factory manual alone.

How often is the technical data updated?

We review technical data on a continuous basis, with major updates annually. Each page shows the last verification date in the summary box. If a TSB or recall changes a spec, we update it promptly.

Can I use this data for non‑Land Rover vehicles?

Some pages cover engines shared with Jaguar (AJ‑V8, Ingenium) or BMW (M47/M57), but the site is focused on Land Rover and Range Rover applications. For other brands, see our BMW or Mercedes sites.

What if the spec I need isn't listed?

Use the Diagnostic Wizard to narrow down your engine, or contact us with a request – we add new pages based on demand.
