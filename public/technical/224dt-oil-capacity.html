The 224DT (2.2‑litre Duratorq diesel) takes approximately 6.5 litres of oil with a filter change [PLACEHOLDER — confirm vs OEM workshop data], using a 5W‑30 or 5W‑40 viscosity oil meeting Ford WSS‑M2C913‑C (or later) specification.

  • Manufacturer service interval: 12,000 miles or 12 months (whichever comes first) on qualifying oil [PLACEHOLDER — confirm vs Ford workshop data]
  • Real‑world caveat: the 224DT is sensitive to EGR and turbocharger health, which are affected by oil quality — see the notes below
  • Fitted to Freelander 2 and Evoque L538, as well as some Ford Transit and Mondeo applications
  • Last verified against Ford workshop data: August 2026

Notes & Caveats

The manufacturer's 12,000‑mile service interval assumes normal use with a healthy mix of driving. In real‑world UK conditions – particularly short urban journeys – the 224DT's EGR and turbocharger systems can suffer from soot and oil degradation, leading to premature wear. Independent specialists we've spoken to [EM-INTERVIEW] frequently recommend a 6,000‑mile oil change interval for vehicles used predominantly in stop‑start traffic or towing, regardless of the service computer indication. This practical adjustment helps mitigate the common failure patterns described on our EGR cooler failure page and turbocharger failure page.

For a deeper look at the 224DT's reliability and cost‑of‑ownership, see the 224DT Engine Knowledge Centre.

Common Mistakes

  • Using the wrong viscosity grade. 5W‑30 is recommended for most UK use; 5W‑40 may be used in warmer climates but check your handbook.
  • Ignoring Ford specification. Using oil that only meets viscosity but not the Ford WSS‑M2C913‑C approval can lead to DPF and EGR issues.
  • Trusting the service computer unconditionally. It cannot detect your driving pattern; short trips demand more frequent changes.
  • Confusing 224DT with other Duratorq variants. The 2.2‑litre engine is different from the 2.0‑litre or 2.4‑litre – check your engine code via our Engine Code Guide.

How to Verify on the Actual Vehicle

The oil filler cap and dipstick are clearly marked and accessible from the top of the engine bay. Confirm your exact engine code by locating the engine number stamp – see our Engine Number Location guide. Early and late production runs of the 224DT may have minor running changes affecting oil specification; always refer to the latest Ford / JLR service bulletin for your VIN.

Why This Specification Matters

Using the correct oil grade and Ford specification is not optional on the 224DT. The engine's turbocharger, EGR system, and DPF are all sensitive to oil viscosity and ash content. Incorrect oil can lead to increased wear, EGR cooler failure, turbocharger oil starvation, and costly repairs – far exceeding the cost of proper maintenance. The 5W‑30 grade is specified for its cold‑start flow characteristics, protecting the turbo during the critical warm‑up phase on cold UK mornings. For a full breakdown of the failure modes linked to poor oil maintenance, visit our 224DT failure patterns page (when available) or the 224DT symptom hub.
