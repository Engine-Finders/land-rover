Technical Service Bulletins (TSBs) – Not Recalls

A Technical Service Bulletin is not a safety recall. It is a communication from JLR to dealers about a known issue that may affect some vehicles, typically for non‑safety concerns. Repairs are usually chargeable unless the vehicle is under warranty.

How to Check Your Freelander for Outstanding Recalls

  1. Find your VIN — located on the driver's side windscreen lower corner, on the driver's door pillar, or in the service book. Use our VIN Decoder tool to decode it.
  2. Check the DVSA recall checker — visit the official DVSA website and enter your number plate or VIN. It's free and takes less than a minute [THIRD‑PARTY].
  3. Contact a JLR dealer — they can run a full history check and confirm if any outstanding recalls apply to your specific VIN.
  4. Check with JLR's own portal — the manufacturer's website has a recall checker that cross‑references your VIN against all known campaigns.

Recalls are free to remedy, regardless of the vehicle's age or warranty status. If an outstanding recall exists, the repair is carried out at no cost to the owner [THIRD‑PARTY].

What Isn't Covered by a Recall

  • Wear and tear items — brake pads, discs, clutch, tyres and batteries are not covered by recalls, even if they contribute to a safety‑related fault.
  • Known faults without a safety case — many common issues (e.g., K‑Series head gasket failure on L314 1.8 petrol) are not recalls unless they pose a direct safety risk. They may be covered by TSBs or goodwill, but not by mandatory recall campaigns.
  • Out‑of‑date software — unless a specific recall requires a software update, engine ECU updates are usually chargeable outside warranty.
  • Issues caused by non‑JLR maintenance — if an aftermarket part or non‑approved service has affected a component, the recall may not apply or may be invalidated.

If you're unsure whether a specific fault is covered, speak to a JLR dealer or a vetted independent specialist.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if my Freelander has any outstanding recalls?

Use the VIN Decoder to get your exact model details, then check the DVSA recall checker or contact a JLR dealer.

Are recall repairs free even if my Freelander is out of warranty?

Yes, safety recalls are always free regardless of vehicle age or mileage.

What's the difference between a TSB and a recall?

A recall is issued when a safety defect is confirmed — the repair is mandatory and free. A TSB is an advisory notice to dealers about known issues; repairs may be chargeable unless under warranty.

Can I check recalls by number plate?

Yes, the DVSA recall checker allows you to search by registration plate — it's the quickest way to see if your vehicle has any outstanding campaigns.

What if my Freelander has an engine-related TSB but no recall?

Common engine issues like K‑Series head gasket failure or 224DT timing chain tensioner wear may have TSBs but not safety recalls. Speak to your dealer or a vetted specialist for advice on goodwill or cost‑effective repair options.
