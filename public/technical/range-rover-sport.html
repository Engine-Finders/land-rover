Technical Service Bulletins (TSBs) are not safety recalls but address common engineering issues. For example, JLR issued TSBs for 306DT crankshaft bearing diagnostics and Ingenium diesel timing chain tensioner wear. These are not free repairs but provide guidance for specialists.

How to Check Your Range Rover Sport for Outstanding Recalls

The only reliable way to check for outstanding recalls is to use your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN). The VIN is a 17-character code found on the driver's side windscreen pillar, inside the door shut, or on the V5C registration document.

  1. Visit the DVSA recall checker — gov.uk/check-vehicle-recall — enter your VIN and number plate.
  2. Use the JLR owner portal — create an account at landrover.co.uk/owners and add your vehicle to see all outstanding campaigns.
  3. Contact a JLR main dealer — any franchised dealer can run your VIN against the JLR global recall database.

If your vehicle has an outstanding recall, the repair is free of charge at any JLR dealer, regardless of service history or warranty status. You are not required to have your vehicle serviced at the dealer to have recall work performed.

What a Recall Does Not Cover

Recalls are issued only for safety-related defects that affect a significant number of vehicles. They are not a warranty extension and do not cover:

  • Wear and tear items — brakes, suspension bushes, tyres, clutch, etc.
  • Known design weaknesses that do not pose an immediate safety risk — for example, 306DT crankshaft bearing failure or Ingenium oil dilution are not recall items, even though they are costly and common.
  • Maintenance items such as timing chains, oil pumps, EGR valves, and DPF systems — unless a specific safety defect is identified.
  • Cosmetic or infotainment issues — software glitches, screen delamination, etc., unless they affect safety systems.

FAQ — Range Rover Sport Recalls

  • How do I find out if my Range Rover Sport has an outstanding recall? Enter your VIN on the DVSA recall checker at gov.uk/check-vehicle-recall. Alternatively, use the JLR owner portal or ask a main dealer.
  • Will a recall repair cost me anything? No — recall work is performed free of charge by any authorised JLR dealer, regardless of warranty or service history.
  • Can I drive my Range Rover Sport if it has an outstanding recall? It depends on the severity. For airbag or fire-risk recalls, you should book the repair immediately. For minor software updates, you can drive normally until the appointment. Always follow the advice given in the recall letter.
  • What is the difference between a recall and a Technical Service Bulletin (TSB)? A recall is a safety-related campaign that is mandatory and free. A TSB is a manufacturer advisory for known engineering issues — it provides guidance to mechanics but does not entitle you to a free repair.
  • Do recalls apply to my imported Range Rover Sport? Usually yes, but you must check with JLR UK. Some recalls are region-specific. Provide your VIN to a UK dealer to confirm.
  • Can I check recalls without the VIN? No — the VIN is essential. If you don't have access to the vehicle, the V5C document contains the VIN.
