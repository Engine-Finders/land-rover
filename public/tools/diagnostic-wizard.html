What Your Diagnostic Results Mean

The likely fault(s) are ranked by frequency in our proprietary 2025 enquiry dataset, combined with expert‑validated failure patterns. The affected engines are those most commonly associated with that fault.

The typical repair cost is a UK independent specialist estimate (parts and labour) based on actual supplier quotes [EM‑QUOTE]. Main dealer costs can be 30–50% higher.

Use the links to our Failure Pages for detailed symptom progression, root cause, diagnostic steps, and a full repair‑or‑replace verdict using the 60% threshold.

Where Our Diagnostic Data Comes From

The symptom‑to‑fault mappings are drawn from three sources:

  • 24,650 real UK quote requests from EnginesMarket.co.uk and EngineFinders.co.uk in 2025 [EM‑VERIFIED] — we analysed the free‑text problem descriptions to identify common symptom patterns.
  • Supplier quote notes — mechanics listing specific failures when quoting for replacement engines.
  • Post‑sale customer surveys (100+ respondents) and manufacturer TSBs, cross‑referenced with public forums.

Full methodology is available on our Data Methodology page. All data is independently vetted by our panel of Land Rover specialists — see our specialist vetting and data verification standards.
