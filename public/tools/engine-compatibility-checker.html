What Your Compatibility Results Mean

The compatibility verdict tells you if the engine was ever factory‑fitted to your model. But compatibility isn't just about bolting it in — our fitment notes cover ECU, gearbox, and mounting considerations.

  • ✅ Compatible — This engine was factory‑fitted to this model. It should be a straight swap with the correct ECU and ancillaries.
  • ⚠️ Compatible with modifications — This engine wasn't factory‑fitted, but can be made to fit with custom mounts, ECU work, or other modifications.
  • 🔴 Not compatible — This engine was never fitted to this model and significant custom work would be required. Not recommended.

For full fitment details, including chassis codes, years, and ECU variants, see our Fitment Matrix or the individual Engine Knowledge Centres.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the engine compatibility checker free?

Yes, completely free with no email required to see your result.

How accurate is the compatibility check?

Very high for factory‑fitted combinations. For non‑factory swaps, the tool provides guidance based on specialist knowledge and 2025 enquiry patterns — but always get a professional assessment before committing.

What does "compatible with modifications" mean?

It means the engine wasn't factory‑fitted to your model, but can be made to fit with custom engine mounts, ECU reprogramming, or other modifications. This is for experienced specialists only.

Can I swap a petrol engine into a diesel Land Rover?

Yes, but it's a major project requiring fuel system replacement, ECU changes, and many other modifications. The tool will flag this as a non‑factory combination with extensive modification notes.

Where can I find more detailed fitment information?

Our Fitment Matrix covers every model, engine, and chassis combination in full detail. You can also visit the Engine Knowledge Centre for each engine.
