What Your Engine Value Estimate Means

The used value is what a breaker, specialist or private buyer would typically pay for your engine in its current condition — based on actual 2025 supplier quote data from our CRM. The reconditioned value is the price a specialist would ask for a professionally rebuilt unit of the same type.

The scrap‑vs‑sell verdict uses the same 60% repair‑to‑value framework published on our Decision Framework page. If the used value is less than 60% of the reconditioned value, selling as‑is or scrapping may be more sensible than investing in a rebuild.

For a deeper dive into your engine's specific value, see our replacement cost by engine code guide or the reconditioned vs used vs rebuilt comparison.

Where Our Engine Value Data Comes From

Value estimates behind this tool are drawn from the same 2025 UK enquiry dataset that powers every other page on this site — 24,650 unique quote requests from EnginesMarket.co.uk and EngineFinders.co.uk, combined. Full detail on our Data Methodology page.

For each engine code, we cross‑reference used and reconditioned supply costs from supplier quotes, adjust for condition and mileage based on market observation, and apply the 60% framework to give you a practical verdict. All figures are independently vetted by our panel of Land Rover specialists — learn more about our specialist vetting process and data verification standards.

Frequently Asked Questions About Engine Value

Is the engine value checker free?

Yes, completely free with no email required to see your result.

How accurate is the value estimate?

Very high for the most common Land Rover engine codes — our estimates are based on real 2025 supplier quotes and enquiry data. For less common codes, we provide a market‑informed range using the same methodology. Always get a professional quote before selling.

What factors affect my engine's value most?

Condition is the biggest factor — a seized engine is worth 30–50% of a running unit. Mileage matters too: engines under 60,000 miles command a premium, while those over 150,000 see a significant discount. The specific engine code also drives value — high‑demand codes like 306DT and 204DTD retain more value.

Where can I sell my Land Rover engine?

Specialist breakers, recon engine suppliers, and independent Land Rover workshops are the main buyers. Our network of 12 vetted JLR specialists can also provide quotes — use the registration lookup to get matched with the right specialist for your engine.

How does the 60% scrap threshold work?

The 60% threshold comes from our Decision Framework: if the cost of a reconditioned engine exceeds 60% of your vehicle's current market value, scrapping the vehicle often makes more financial sense than repairing. This tool applies the same logic to your engine's value.
