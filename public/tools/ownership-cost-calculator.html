What Your Ownership Cost Results Mean

The annual running cost includes fuel, VED (road tax), insurance, and servicing — but excludes depreciation. The total TCO (Total Cost of Ownership) includes depreciation over the ownership period, giving you a true picture of what it costs to own the vehicle.

The cost‑per‑mile is the total annual cost divided by annual mileage — a useful benchmark for comparing vehicles. The verdict rates the model as Affordable, Moderate, or Expensive to run, based on the 60% threshold framework used across this site.

Where Our Ownership Cost Data Comes From

Cost data is sourced from:

  • Insurance groups: Based on model‑specific insurance group data, cross‑referenced with 2025 quotes
  • Service costs: Independent specialist rates from 24,650 quote requests [EM‑VERIFIED]

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ownership cost calculator free?

Yes, completely free with no email required to see your result.

How accurate are the cost estimates?

Very high — we use 2025 UK market data, real fuel prices, DVLA tax bands, and service costs from 24,650 real enquiries [EM‑VERIFIED]. However, individual costs vary by location, driving style and vehicle condition.

What's included in the total cost of ownership?

TCO includes fuel, VED, insurance, servicing, and depreciation over the ownership period. It excludes financing costs, repairs, and optional extras.

How does depreciation affect my costs?

Depreciation is often the largest ownership cost. We use model‑specific depreciation rates from 2025 market data to give you a realistic estimate of how much value your Land Rover will lose.

Can I use this for any Land Rover model?

Yes, we cover all major models — Defender, Discovery, Discovery Sport, Evoque, Freelander 2, Range Rover, Range Rover Sport, and Velar.
