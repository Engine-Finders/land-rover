What Your Verdict Means

The repair‑to‑value ratio is the repair cost divided by the vehicle's current market value. Our verdict is based on the 60% threshold used across the Land Rover and Jaguar sites, reflecting genuine parts and labour baselines from our enquiry data.

  • ✅ Repair — cost is ≤ 60% of vehicle value. Financially sensible to repair.
  • ⚠️ Replace / Consider Options — cost is between 60% and 100% of value. Replacement may be borderline; evaluate condition and ownership horizon.
  • 🔴 Scrap / Part‑out — cost exceeds vehicle value. Repair is not financially justifiable.

For a deeper dive, see our Decision Framework and replacement cost by engine code guides.

Where Our Data Comes From

The repair‑to‑value threshold (60%) is derived from our 2025 UK enquiry dataset — 24,650 unique quote requests from EnginesMarket.co.uk and EngineFinders.co.uk. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the repair vs replace calculator free?

Yes, completely free with no email required to see your result.

Where can I find my vehicle's current market value?

Use our Engine Value Checker for a quick estimate, or check Auto Trader, Parkers, and similar sources for similar models. For an accurate value, get a professional appraisal.

What if the repair cost is more than the vehicle value?

If the repair cost exceeds 100% of the vehicle's value, it's almost always better to scrap or part out the vehicle. However, rare or sentimental vehicles may justify the spend — the tool gives guidance, not a hard rule.

Does this tool work for any Land Rover model?

Yes, the 60% threshold applies to all Land Rover models. For specific engine costs, we provide reference data for the most common codes. For others, use the general guidance.

How accurate are the engine‑specific cost references?

They are based on real 2025 UK supplier quotes [EM‑QUOTE] and are updated regularly. They serve as a benchmark — always get a quote for your specific vehicle.
