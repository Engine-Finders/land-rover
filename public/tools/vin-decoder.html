What the VIN Decoder Results Mean

The engine code is the definitive code for your vehicle's primary engine — if your VIN corresponds to a model that spans two engine generations (e.g., certain D300 and D200 vehicles, fully covered in our AJ20D6 fitment guide), we'll flag this and point you to the engine number location guide to confirm.

The verdict uses the same 60% repair‑to‑value framework published openly on our Decision Framework page — it's a starting guide based on typical values for your model, not a substitute for your own vehicle's specific valuation. If the result shows a potential fault, you can dive straight into the relevant Failure Page for detailed diagnosis and cost breakdowns.

Frequently Asked Questions About VIN Decoding

Is the VIN decoder tool free?

Yes, completely free with no email required to see your result.

How accurate is the VIN decoding?

Very high for the primary engine fitted to your VIN. For VINs that correspond to a model spanning two engine generations across their production run, we show both possibilities and recommend confirming against your engine number stamp.

Where can I find my Land Rover VIN?

You'll find the VIN on the driver's side windscreen (viewable from outside), on the driver's door pillar, or stamped into the chassis under the bonnet. Our engine number location guide also shows where to find the engine number.

What if my VIN isn't recognised in the demo?

You can use our full Fitment Matrix to identify your engine manually by model, generation and build year, or use the registration lookup if you have the UK plate.

Can I decode a VIN for a Jaguar or BMW using this tool?

This tool is specific to Land Rover VINs. For Jaguar, use our sister site Jaguar VIN Decoder; for BMW, visit BMW VIN Decoder.
