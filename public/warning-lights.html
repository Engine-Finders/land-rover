Engine & Emissions Warning Lights

The most common dashboard alerts – these lights usually point to engine or emissions system issues.

Engine Management Light (Amber)

ECU has logged a fault code – emissions, sensor, or engine performance issue.

DPF Warning Light

Diesel Particulate Filter blockage or failed regeneration.

Glow Plug Light

Glow plug system fault – often a simple relay or fuse issue.

AdBlue Warning

AdBlue level low, system fault, or NOx sensor issues.

Restricted Performance

ECU has limited power to protect the engine – often turbo or fuel-related.

For fault code equivalents, visit our Fault Codes index.

Fluid & Temperature Warning Lights

These lights indicate problems with engine oil, coolant, or temperature – often urgent.

Oil Pressure Warning

Low oil pressure – stop immediately to avoid engine seizure.

Coolant Temperature Warning

Engine overheating – pull over and allow to cool.

Battery Warning Light

Charging system fault – alternator or battery issue.

If you see a red oil pressure or coolant light, stop driving immediately – see our engine overheating guide.

Suspension & Driveline Warning Lights

Land Rover's air suspension and driveline systems have their own specific alerts.

Suspension Fault

Air suspension system fault – compressor, valve block, or height sensor.

Transmission Fault

Gearbox or transfer case issue – often accompanied by limp mode.

For driveline problems, see our transfer box and ZF gearbox guides.
