What the Coolant Temperature Warning Means

The coolant temperature warning — typically a red thermometer icon or the words "Coolant Temperature" on the dashboard — indicates the engine coolant has exceeded its normal operating range (usually above 120°C). This is a critical safety system designed to alert you before metal components expand beyond tolerance and cause permanent damage. The warning is triggered by the engine coolant temperature sensor, which sends a signal to the ECU when the temperature rises above a pre-set threshold. On Land Rover and Range Rover models, especially those with 3.0 SDV6 and Ingenium engines, overheating is a known risk if cooling system components are neglected [PATTERN DATA].

When this warning appears, the engine is already in distress. The oil may break down, the cylinder head can warp, and the head gasket can fail. The longer you drive, the more damage accumulates — and the higher the repair bill.

Amber vs Red — What Changes

🟡 Amber / Yellow Warning

  • Temperature is rising — still below critical threshold
  • Often appears with low coolant level or a failing thermostat
  • You have a short window to act — pull over and investigate
  • Typical fix: top up coolant, check for leaks, or replace thermostat

🔴 Red Warning — Stop Immediately

  • Overheating has occurred — damage is imminent
  • Continued driving can warp the cylinder head, blow the head gasket, or seize the engine
  • Stop and turn off the engine as soon as it is safe
  • Typical fix: tow to a specialist, assess damage, repair cooling system and replace affected components

If you see a red coolant temperature warning, do not attempt to drive to a garage. Call for recovery. This is not a suggestion — it is the single most important rule for protecting your engine.

What To Do In the Next 24 Hours

1

Stop and turn off the engine

If the red warning appears, pull over safely, turn off the engine, and do not restart. Opening the bonnet can help dissipate heat, but do not remove the coolant cap while the engine is hot — severe burns risk.

2

Check coolant level (when cold)

Only after the engine has cooled for at least an hour, check the coolant expansion tank level. Low level suggests a leak; if it's empty, you have a significant coolant loss issue.

3

Look for visible leaks

Inspect under the vehicle for puddles of coolant (bright green, pink, or orange). Common leak points: radiator, hoses, water pump, and the coolant crossover pipes on V6/V8 engines.

4

Don't just top up and drive

Topping up the coolant may temporarily lower the temperature, but if there's an underlying leak or pump failure, the warning will return — and the damage may already be done.

5

Get it recovered to a specialist

Do not drive the vehicle. Call for recovery to a specialist who can pressure-test the cooling system, identify the leak, and check for engine damage.

Can You Drive With the Coolant Temperature Warning On?

🟡 Amber — Short Distance, Caution

  • If you have an amber warning, you have a brief window
  • Pull over as soon as it's safe, check coolant, and decide
  • If you must move the vehicle, drive gently and monitor temperature
  • Better to recover than risk overheating

⛔ Red — Do Not Drive

  • Stop immediately — the engine is overheating
  • Driving any further risks warping the cylinder head, blowing the head gasket, or seizing the engine
  • Call for recovery — do not attempt to drive to a garage
  • If you have no other choice, drive only as far as the nearest safe stopping point

The exception is if the coolant temperature warning appears alongside an oil pressure warning or engine management light — in those cases, stop and recover regardless.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the coolant temperature warning mean on a Land Rover?

The coolant temperature warning indicates the engine coolant has exceeded safe operating temperature. It is a critical alert — continued driving can cause severe engine damage. If the light is red, stop immediately.

Can I reset the coolant temperature warning myself?

The warning will clear once the engine cools down and the temperature sensor reads a normal value. However, if the underlying cause (e.g., leak, stuck thermostat) isn't fixed, the warning will return. Do not ignore it.

How much does it cost to fix a coolant temperature warning on a Land Rover?

A simple thermostat replacement costs £200–£500. A water pump failure can cost £400–£1,200. If overheating has caused head gasket damage, costs can exceed £1,500. A full engine replacement for severe overheating can be £4,500–£9,500+ [EM-QUOTE].

Can I drive with the coolant temperature warning on?

Amber: you have a short window — pull over and investigate. Red: do not drive — stop immediately and call for recovery. Driving with a red warning is one of the fastest ways to destroy your engine.

What should I do if my Land Rover overheats?

Pull over safely, turn off the engine, and open the bonnet to help dissipate heat. Do not remove the coolant cap while hot. Once the engine has cooled, check coolant level and look for leaks. Call for recovery and have the vehicle inspected by a specialist.
