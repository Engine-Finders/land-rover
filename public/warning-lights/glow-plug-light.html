What the Glow Plug / Preheat Light Means

The glow plug light — typically an amber icon of a coiled spring or the word "Preheat" — is part of the diesel engine's cold-start system. When you turn the key to the "on" position, the ECU powers the glow plugs to heat the combustion chambers, making cold starting easier. The light should come on for a few seconds and then go out — this is normal operation.

If the light stays on, flashes, or comes on while the engine is already running, it means the ECU has detected a fault in the preheat system or a related engine management issue. On Land Rover and Range Rover diesels — from the Ingenium 2.0 to the 3.0 SDV6 — glow plug faults are common, especially on higher-mileage vehicles [PATTERN DATA]. A flashing glow plug light can also indicate a fuel injection or timing issue, which is more serious.

Amber vs Flashing — What Changes

🟡 Amber (Solid) — Preheat Fault

  • Stays on after startup — glow plug or relay fault
  • May cause hard starting in cold weather
  • Typically safe to drive, but may affect cold-start performance
  • Typical fix: glow plug replacement or relay check (£100–£600)

🔶 Flashing — Wider Engine Fault

  • Flashing while running — often indicates a fuel system, injection, or timing fault
  • May be accompanied by engine management light or restricted performance
  • Stop and recover if accompanied by other warnings or rough running
  • Typical fix: diagnostic scan, injector or sensor replacement (£200–£1,500)

If the glow plug light flashes while driving and the engine is running roughly, stop and recover. This can be a sign of a serious fuel or timing issue that could cause engine damage.

What To Do In the Next 24 Hours

1

Note the behaviour

Does the light stay on after startup, flash while driving, or only appear during preheat? This context helps narrow the cause.

2

Check for other warnings

If the glow plug light is flashing alongside the engine management light or restricted performance, stop and recover.

3

Don't just clear the code

Clearing the code without fixing the underlying cause is a short-term fix — the warning will return, and the problem may worsen.

4

Check battery and charging

Low battery voltage can cause glow plug and relay faults. A simple battery test can rule this out.

5

Get a diagnostic scan

If the light stays on or flashes, a diagnostic scan is needed. Most independents can read glow plug-related codes (e.g., P0670–P0676). This is the only way to pinpoint the fault.

6

Narrow it down

Can You Drive With the Glow Plug Light On?

✅ Solid Amber — Yes, With Caution

  • If the light stays on after startup, you can drive — but cold starts may be harder
  • If the light is solid and stays on, book a glow plug check soon
  • Avoid long journeys if the vehicle is hard to start

⛔ Flashing — Stop If Accompanied by Other Warnings

  • If the light is flashing and the engine is running roughly, stop and recover
  • If it's flashing but the engine runs normally, drive directly to a garage
  • Flashing can indicate a fuel or timing issue — don't ignore it

The exception is if the glow plug light appears alongside an oil pressure warning, coolant temperature warning, or any knocking noise — in those cases, stop and recover regardless.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the glow plug warning light mean on a Land Rover?

The glow plug warning light normally indicates the preheat system is active. If it stays on, flashes, or comes on while driving, it means the ECU has detected a fault — either a failed glow plug, relay issue, or a wider engine management fault.

Can I reset the glow plug warning myself?

You can clear the code with an OBD-II scanner, but the warning will return if the underlying cause isn't fixed. A diagnostic scan is the correct first step to identify the fault.

How much does it cost to fix a glow plug warning on a Land Rover?

A single glow plug replacement costs £100–£300. A full set costs £300–£600. If the relay or control module has failed, costs can reach £200–£600. If the flashing light indicates a fuel or timing issue, costs can exceed £1,500 [EM-QUOTE].

Can I drive with the glow plug light on?

Solid amber: yes, but book a check soon. Flashing: if the engine runs smoothly, drive directly to a garage. If the engine runs roughly or there are other warnings, stop and recover.

What causes the glow plug light to flash while driving?

A flashing glow plug light while driving often indicates a fuel injection or engine timing fault. It can be caused by a faulty crankshaft sensor, injector failure, or timing chain stretch. This is more serious than a simple glow plug fault.
